# Provisional assumptions

These are demo choices, not validated production policy.

1. Insurance, a fictional financial pack and a demo agreement apply to all companies. They run independently of skill qualification.
2. All administrative approvals precede vendor creation; vendor → portal → induction follows the brief's provisional sequence.
3. Training explicitly confirms contractor induction; self-completion alone does not establish the training readiness fact.
4. HVAC and Plumbing are a flat catalog. Valid demo license plus Field Manager approval stands in for competency standards.
5. Predefined fixtures represent document submission/verification. No actual legal or financial validity is inferred.
6. Explicit Run controls stand in for asynchronous workers and make failures/retries observable.
7. Human work has a two-demo-day due date; one reminder when due, one escalation two days later to Contractor Relations. Not a production SLA.
8. Eligibility is derived readiness. Activation publication is a separate acknowledgement needed to complete onboarding. Loss immediately suspends that projection.
9. Additional skills require Active and Ready. Approved/in-progress duplicates are blocked; a rejected/invalidated skill can be requested again while another skill keeps the company eligible.
10. Explicit invalidation demonstrates readiness reevaluation. No periodic revalidation or reinstatement policy is invented.
11. Each submission creates a company; identity deduplication rules were not supplied. Iteration 3 supports concurrent tabs on the exact origin and Chrome profile through Web Locks. No authentication or cross-device synchronization is added.
12. Running Iteration 1 instances upgrade in place with a migration trace. This is not production Temporal versioning.

# Unresolved product questions

- What are real coverage thresholds, license jurisdictions, expiry policies and applicability exceptions?
- Which financial checks and remediation roles are required?
- Are agreements company-level, trade-specific, regional or versioned by contract?
- Is training self-attested, instructor-confirmed, assessed or synchronized from another system?
- What follows rejection of the first/only skill: appeal, new skill, reapplication or closure? Onboarding currently stays non-ready and waiting.
- What reinstatement/requalification follows invalidation of the only skill? This iteration supports removal/suspension, not an invented reinstatement path.
- Should additional skills be allowed before activation? Can a request be withdrawn?
- Which readiness losses need external deactivation acknowledgement, and what happens to open jobs?
- What are actual due dates, calendars, escalation recipients, templates and retry/backoff limits?
- What are production rules for company matching, assignment, evidence retention, access control and concurrency?
- Which workflow and event contracts should be versioned independently in a Temporal deployment?

The supplied brief ends mid-sentence after asking for assumptions and unresolved questions to be captured separately. This document makes that distinction without guessing omitted requirements.
